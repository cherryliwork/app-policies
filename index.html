English

Rulivol sets device media volume according to the app in front, time slots and audio output. This policy explains data processing by the app, voluntary email support and this policy website. The developer and support contact are listed at the top of this page.

Accessibility and app information

After you agree to the disclosure and enable its Accessibility service yourself, Rulivol uses the focused app’s package name, window type and focus state to identify the current app and choose a volume rule. It does not read screen text, passwords, typed content, notifications or messages, take screenshots, or tap or type for you.

The app picker queries launcher apps’ names, package names, icons and categories on the device so you can select groups, see app names and use the smart catalog. Rulivol does not upload this information or a complete app list to a server.

Readable current records and their recovery copies are pruned when the app reads or writes them. If you do not use the app again, it does not keep waking the device in the background just to clean these records; data may remain until the next cleanup, clearing storage or uninstalling.

If a data file is corrupt or unreadable, a private recovery copy may be kept to avoid overwriting potentially recoverable data. Such copies may contain older records and remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. They are not uploaded or included in a settings export.

Automatic learning is off by default. Turning it off stops automatic adoption of rules; local habit suggestions may still use your manual adjustments. You can forget a habit or an app’s samples in the Habits screen. This also removes corresponding learned time slots while retaining manually created groups and time slots.

Sharing, backups and network access

Rulivol has no advertising SDK, third-party tracking or analytics uploads. It does not send rules, detected apps, activity, habits or Bluetooth names to a developer server. This closed-test build disables purchasing and runtime network access and does not charge you.

App data is excluded from automatic cloud backup and system device transfer. A settings export is written only to your selected location and contains groups, time slots, settings and any app package names or Bluetooth names configured in them. It excludes activity, manual adjustment samples, daily statistics, purchase tokens, consent and automation state. Your chosen file or cloud storage provider may sync the exported file under its own settings. Rulivol cannot delete those exported copies after you uninstall it.

Import reads a settings file that you choose. After an import, automation and the imported groups remain off; review the settings before enabling them. An imported file does not grant Accessibility consent or turn on the service for you.

Google Play and Android may process distribution, installation and system diagnostic data under their own terms; see Google’s privacy policy. These platform services do not mean that the app uploads the local records described above to Google.

Stopping and deleting local data

You can pause automatic volume until you turn it back on, or disable the Accessibility service in Android settings, to stop the related app observations and automatic volume changes. Pausing does not erase stored data.

Clearing the Activity screen deletes only activity entries, not habits or settings. The Habits screen lets you forget the relevant samples and learned time slots. Android’s Clear storage or uninstalling removes all Rulivol private app data. Export any rules you wish to keep before doing so. Delete any export copies you saved at their storage location.

Email support and this policy website

No Rulivol account is required. If you email cherryli@suyoua.com, we receive your email address and any name, message text, screenshots or other attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue you report. Send only information needed for that purpose. We retain this correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it after completion when it is no longer needed. You can use the same email address to request deletion. Your email provider handles the message under its own terms; the Rulivol app does not upload feedback automatically.

When you view this policy on GitHub Pages, GitHub hosts the page and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if visitors are not signed in. See About GitHub Pages and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This policy page contains no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts.

Future releases and policy changes

A later purchase-enabled release may receive Google Play purchase receipts, product and purchase states, signatures and purchase tokens for on-device signature verification, unlocking purchased features and acknowledging purchases with Google Play. Google Play handles payment details. This flow is not active in the current closed-test build.

Changes to data use, retention, purchasing or any future server or diagnostic uploads will be described in an updated policy on this page, with a revised effective date. The policy accompanying the version you use will explain the processing that applies to that version.

无障碍与应用信息

在你同意说明并自行开启系统无障碍服务后，Rulivol 使用焦点应用的包名、窗口类型及焦点状态，判断当前应用并匹配音量规则。它不读取屏幕文字、密码、输入内容、通知或消息，不截图，不替你点击或输入。

应用选择器在本机查询有桌面启动入口的应用名称、包名、图标和类别，以便选择分组、显示名称及匹配智能目录。Rulivol 不把这些信息或完整应用列表上传到服务器。

分享、备份与网络

Rulivol 没有广告 SDK、第三方追踪或应用内分析上报，不向开发者服务器上传规则、应用识别、动态、习惯或蓝牙名称。本轮封测构建禁用购买和运行时网络访问，不会发起收费。

应用数据不参与自动云备份或系统设备迁移。你主动导出的设置文件只写到你选择的位置，包含分组、时段、设置以及其中配置的应用包名和蓝牙名称；不包含动态、手动调整样本、每日统计、购买 token、授权或自动化运行状态。你选用的文件或云存储服务可能按自身设置同步文件。Rulivol 无法在卸载后删除这些导出副本。

导入只读取你选择的设置文件。导入后自动化和导入的分组保持关闭；请先核对设置，再自行启用。导入文件不会代替你同意无障碍说明或开启系统服务。

Google Play 或 Android 可能按自身服务条款处理应用分发、安装和系统诊断信息。其处理方式见 Google 隐私政策；这不表示 Rulivol 将上述本地记录上传给 Google。

停止和删除

你可以在 Rulivol 总开关选择「直到我重新打开」来暂停自动化，也可以在 Android 无障碍设置关闭服务，停止相关读取和自动调音。暂停不会自动删除已保存的数据。

「动态」页的清除操作只删除动态，不等于删除习惯或设置。「习惯」页可忘掉相关样本和学来的时段。Android 应用信息中的「清除存储」或卸载会删除 Rulivol 的全部应用私有数据；需要保留规则时应先导出。自行保存的导出文件需在其存储位置删除。
